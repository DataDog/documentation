This partial contains instructions for enabling RUM with the Roku SDK.
-->

RUM is enabled automatically when you initialize the SDK. No further action is needed.

The Roku SDK doesn't track views or actions automatically. After you initialize the SDK, see [Track Navigation][1] and [Track User Interactions][2] to instrument your channel.

[2]: /real_user_monitoring/setup/enable_rum/track_user_interactions/?platform=roku

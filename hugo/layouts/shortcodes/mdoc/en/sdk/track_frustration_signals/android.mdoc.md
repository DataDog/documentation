The Mobile RUM SDK automatically detects frustration signals with no additional configuration required.

## Error taps

A user taps on an element, and at least one error occurs during the action's duration or within 100 ms after the action ends.

For more information about frustration signals and how to use them, see [Frustration Signals][1].

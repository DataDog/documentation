## Overview

Error Tracking processes errors collected from the iOS SDK, including crashes, app hangs, and watchdog terminations.

Your crash reports appear in [{{< ui >}}Error Tracking{{< /ui >}}][1].

## Setup

To enable iOS Crash Reporting and Error Tracking, see [Track errors and crashes][2].

For symbolication, deobfuscation, and other advanced Error Tracking details, see [iOS Crash Reporting and Error Tracking][3].

## Overview

Error Tracking processes errors collected from the Kotlin Multiplatform SDK, including crashes and ANRs on Android, and crashes and app hangs on iOS.

Your crash reports appear in [{{< ui >}}Error Tracking{{< /ui >}}][1].

## Setup

To enable Kotlin Multiplatform Crash Reporting and Error Tracking, see [Track errors and crashes][2].

For deobfuscation and other advanced Error Tracking details, see [Kotlin Multiplatform Crash Reporting and Error Tracking][3].

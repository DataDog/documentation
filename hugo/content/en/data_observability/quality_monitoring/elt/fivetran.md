Datadog's Fivetran integration helps data teams understand how data flows from external sources into their data warehouses and trace quality issues back to upstream sources.

Lineage is derived from your data warehouse, so you also need at least one [supported data warehouse destination][2] connected to Datadog.

See the [Fivetran integration][1] documentation for setup and configuration.

Datadog's Hightouch integration helps data teams understand how data flows from their data warehouse to downstream business tools and trace quality issues back to upstream sources.

Lineage is derived from your data warehouse, so you also need at least one [supported data warehouse source][2] connected to Datadog.

See the [Hightouch integration][1] documentation for setup and configuration.

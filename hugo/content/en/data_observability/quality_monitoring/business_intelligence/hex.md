Datadog's Hex integration helps data teams view end-to-end lineage between warehouse tables and Hex projects.

Lineage is derived from your data warehouse, so you also need at least one [supported data warehouse destination][2] connected to Datadog.

See the [Hex integration][1] documentation for setup and configuration.

## Overview

{{< img src="observability_pipelines/packs/gigamon_gigavue_ami_cef.png" alt="The Gigamon GigaVUE AMI CEF pack" style="width:25%;" >}}

Gigamon AMI captures application traffic metadata from GigaVUE in CEF.

What this pack does:

- Parses CEF logs into structured fields
- Extracts byte, packet, and record metrics
- Normalizes flow metadata

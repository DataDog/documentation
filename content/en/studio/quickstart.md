This quickstart walks you through connecting your first application to Datadog Studio. By the end, you have telemetry flowing from your local environment into Studio.

## Prerequisites

- Datadog Studio installed. See [Download and Install][1].
- A Datadog account and credentials.
- A local application you want to instrument.

## Connect your first app

1. Open Datadog Studio and sign in with your Datadog credentials.
2. Select the project folder for the application you want to instrument.
3. Follow the in-app onboarding to connect your app and start sending telemetry.
4. Confirm that data is flowing by viewing your application's traces, logs, and metrics in Studio.
